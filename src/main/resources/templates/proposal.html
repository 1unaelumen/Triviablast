Authors

About The Project

TriviaBlast is an online quiz game that allows users to play both singleplayer and multiplayer matches.

Players can practice individually to gain points or compete against others in multiplayer rooms with classic category-based gameplay.

The questions are provided by the public Open Trivia Database (OpenTDB), which offers a wide range of categories and difficulty levels.

User Roles & Permissions

Player

Players can manage their accounts, play singleplayer games, and view the leaderboard rankings.

Administrator

Administrators can hide, edit, delete, or restore user visibility through the admin system.

Game Modes

Singleplayer Mode

Players can configure the game by selecting the number of questions, category, and difficulty level.

The final score depends on the difficulty and response speed.

Points & Leaderboard

Players earn points according to the game mode and their performance.

Rankings are displayed in the leaderboard, where players appear ordered from highest to lowest score.

Administrators can hide users from the leaderboard when necessary.

Gameplay Flow

  • User registration or login
  • Selecting a game mode
  • Playing the match
  • Calculating points
  • Updating the leaderboard
